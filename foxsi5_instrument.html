FOXSI-5 Instrumentation 🦊⚠️

⚠️ Work In Progress ⚠️

Details on the make-up of the FOXSI-5 instrument. This page will help a user decide what response components they want when using the response files and/or Response-tools code.

Overview

⚠️ Work In Progress ⚠️

The FOXSI-5 instrument is composed of seven independent X-ray telescopes. Each of these seven includes a direct-focusing X-ray optic module, a focal plane detector, and some attenuation material along the optical path. Detectors

⚠️ Work In Progress ⚠️

Three types of detectors were flown on FOXSI-5:

  1. CMOS soft X-ray detectors in positions 0 and 1,

  2. CdTe double-sided strip detectors in positions 2–5,

  3. Timepix detector in position 6.
